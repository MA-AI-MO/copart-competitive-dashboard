SEM Spend: Estimated monthly paid search spend · derived from industry SEM benchmarks and analyst estimates, 2024. Not disclosed by any company. Title Processing: Annual titles processed or facilitated · Copart & IAA = insurance/salvage; Manheim/OpenLane/ACV = wholesale dealer facilitation; CarMax & Carvana = retail consumer title transfers; eBay Motors = N/A.
